# How GardenPost Works

GardenPost uses GardenLands property mailboxes as delivery targets. Mailboxes can be chests, copper chests, or barrels when registered by GardenLands.

Mail delivery is tied to a specific property mailbox. Courier completion and rewards are persisted so the same route cannot be paid twice after a restart.

GardenPost depends on GardenCore for storage and payments and on GardenLands for property and mailbox data.
